Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions regarding software-only digital twin deployment, virtual stations, and data privacy.

Are physical hardware sensors or IoT devices required?
No hardware sensors or IoT devices are required! AETHERIS is a 100% software-powered digital environment twin. It computes high-resolution microclimates, particulate plumes, and thermal gradients using mathematical fluid dynamics, satellite weather models, and open meteorological APIs. You get precision digital twin analytics with zero hardware costs and zero physical maintenance.
How does the GPS Proximity Pairing work?
When you allow location permission, the system takes your device coordinates and computes high-precision Haversine geodesic distance and azimuth bearing to every twin station in real time. It pairs you with the closest node and visualizes a laser vector link on the 2.5D radar canvas.
Can AETHERIS run fully on-premise or offline?
Yes. The backend runs on Java Spring Boot and MySQL (with seamless fallback to embedded H2 if database credentials are not configured). The frontend is completely standalone HTML5/Vanilla JS, allowing deployment in completely air-gapped municipal command rooms.
How are the 'What-If' hazard simulations calculated?
Our atmospheric simulation applies Gaussian plume dispersion physics modulated by live wind speed, elevation variance, and thermal buoyancy. Injected scenarios like heatwave spikes or industrial emissions simulate cascading effects across neighboring nodes.
